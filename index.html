Why you're still stuck

Why what you've tried before didn't stick

You're not here because you haven't put in the effort. You've tried. The problem is that most funnel advice is built for a different kind of business, and you've been trying to make it fit yours.

The problem

DIY funnels built from scattered advice.

A YouTube tutorial here, a free template there, a course you never finished. The result is a funnel built by committee. Nothing connects, and nothing converts.

The problem

Copywriters who didn't understand your offer.

They wrote something polished. It just didn't sound like you, didn't speak to your specific client, and didn't reflect why your approach is different. Generic copy, generic results.

The problem

Tech that was set up but never tested.

The tools were connected, in theory. But no one checked whether the right email goes to the right person at the right time. Broken automations mean broken trust.

The problem

Strategy sessions with no implementation.

You paid for someone to tell you what to build. Then you had to build it yourself, on top of running your business. The plan sat in a Google Doc and quietly became irrelevant.

The problem

Funnels designed for volume, not high-ticket.

Low-ticket and high-ticket funnels are structurally different. A funnel built for a $97 course won't convert a $3K offer, no matter how good the copy is.

The fix isn't trying harder with the same approach. It's building the right infrastructure from the start.

Before you reach out

The things people tell me first…

"The copy won't sound like me."

Before I write a single word, I go deep on your brand voice: your existing content, how your clients describe their problems, and the specific language they use. The copy I deliver sounds like you on your clearest, most confident day. Not like a stranger who read your About page once.

"I've already tried building a funnel. It didn't convert."

A funnel that doesn't convert usually has one of three problems: the strategy is wrong, the copy isn't specific enough, or the tech is broken. What I build is all three, aligned, from the start. If you've already got something in place, we diagnose what's failing and fix it properly, not just tweak a headline and cross our fingers.

"My business is too relationship-based for a funnel."

A funnel isn't a replacement for relationships, it's the thing that makes them more efficient. Instead of manually warming up every lead from zero, your funnel does that first layer of trust-building for you, so by the time someone reaches you, they already feel like they know you.

"I don't have time to be involved in a big project right now."

You don't need to be. I handle the strategy, the writing and the tech. What I need from you is a clear brief at the start, feedback at a couple of checkpoints, and your approval before anything goes live. Most clients tell me it takes far less of their time than they expected.

"I'm not sure I have enough traffic to make a funnel worth it."

A leaky funnel doesn't get better with more traffic, it just loses more people faster. Getting the structure right first means that when you do focus on driving traffic, the infrastructure is ready to handle it.

"What if I invest in this and it still doesn't work?"

Before we agree on anything, I'll tell you honestly whether a funnel build is the right next step or whether something else needs to happen first. If the Revenue System Audit reveals a different bottleneck, like your offer, your pricing or your positioning, I'll say so.

"What does this cost and how long does it take?"

A full funnel build, including strategy, copy, email sequence and tech setup, typically starts at $2,500 depending on scope. Timelines are usually 3–4 weeks from kickoff. If you're not sure what you need yet, the Revenue System Audit ($297) is the right starting point.
