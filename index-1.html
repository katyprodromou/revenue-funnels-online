The reframe

This isn't marketing. Not the way you're picturing it.

If the word marketing makes you wince, good. You are not a marketing business and I am not going to try to turn you into one.

What you actually need is quieter than that. It is making an invisible reputation visible and findable. Taking the thing everyone who has worked with you already knows, and making sure the right new people can find it too.

The bit that stops most people

You don't need to find the words. That's my job.

You are not supposed to be good at describing your own value. Almost nobody is. Even brilliant writers and performers freeze the moment it is their own work. So you do not need to arrive with it figured out. I just need enough raw material from you, the stories, the feedback, the way you talk about it off the cuff, and I find the words accurately.

The things people say first

You might be thinking…

"We're not really a marketing business. That language doesn't fit us."

You are right, and I will not make you into one. None of this is about becoming salesy or chasing an algorithm. It is about making your reputation findable, in your own voice, on your own terms.

"I don't have the words for this, and I'm supposed to be good with words."

That is exactly the point, and exactly my job. The people best at their craft are often the worst at describing it, because they are too close to it. You bring the raw material. I find the words.

"Our website already exists."

It probably does a lovely job as a record of what you have done. This is about turning it into something that also helps a new person understand you and decide to book you, without stripping out what makes it yours.

"I worry we're not polished enough to put ourselves out there."

You do not need to be polished. You need to be visible and clear. We start small and gently, and I promise the goal is never to make you perform a version of yourself that is not real.

"We run pretty lean."

Understood, and respected. We can start with one small, focused piece and see what it moves before you commit to anything bigger. No pressure, no big upfront leap.
