Your work is genuinely good. That was never the question.

But somewhere between the brilliant thing you make and the right new person hearing about it, something goes quiet. The people already in your circle know exactly how good you are. Everyone else? They never get the chance to find out.

Where it gets lost

It's not one big thing. It's lots of small leaks.

  • Your website documents what you have done, but it does not persuade a new person to book you
  • You find it genuinely hard to say what is special about your work, so the pages stay vague
  • Real, glowing feedback is scattered across old emails, doing nothing where nobody can see it
  • You are not quite sure you are "professional enough" to post, so you mostly do not
  • Everything relies on word of mouth, so when the referrals slow, so does everything else

Each one is small. Together, they add up to a reputation that only travels as far as the last person who saw you.

The good news

A visibility problem is a fixable problem.

You do not need more talent. You do not need a full rebrand or to become a "content creator." You need to see, clearly and in one place, where your work is quietly getting lost, and then plug those specific gaps.

Most of the time the fixes are smaller and simpler than you would expect. The proof already exists. The stories already exist. They just need finding, gathering, and pointing in the right direction.
